A mood to interpret, not a look to copy.

Our K-pop-inspired concept combines sheer syrup colors, soft pastels, small bows and pearls, or darker chrome accents. Use it to explore your own concert outfit idea or everyday nail mood. These are creative starting points, not claims about what a particular artist wears.

Nail Art Maker is an independent design tool. It is not affiliated with any artist, entertainment agency or nail product brand.

Build a set one nail at a time.

  • Choose a round, oval, almond, square or coffin shape and adjust the length for the set.
  • Set a color, opacity and finish for each nail. Compare jelly, milky, matte and chrome effects.
  • Layer gradients, French tips and patterns from different design groups.
  • Add bows, pearls or seasonal charms. Adjust their color, position, size and rotation, or drag them to another nail.
  • Save a PNG image for reference and a JSON work file to keep editing later.

What this simulator can help you plan.

Try a milky-pink ribbon set, a nude syrup base with burgundy French tips, or an olive-and-cream winter palette. Comparing one or two changes at a time helps you decide which nails should stay simple and where to place an accent.

The tool uses the available controls and assets; it is not a text-to-image AI generator or a virtual hand try-on. Real gel color, shine and texture vary with products, lighting and application.

Keep an editable copy.

Autosave stays in this browser on this device. Export a work file before clearing browsing data or switching devices. There is no account-based cloud sync. Statistics are optional; declining them does not disable editing.
